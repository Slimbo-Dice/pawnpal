Your Pal is the figure you brought: a mini, a token, a photo of something you care about. They travel a road. Each day brings one event. You roll the d10 to see how it lands.

Will you accept your day's fate, or take a gamble before the day is done?

Most Pals fall. Some last for weeks. A rare few make it to retirement. Either way, their story joins the others when their run ends.
